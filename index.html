About

The Integration Layer

I'm a systems integrator — the person who shows up at the intersection of product, analytics, large-scale implementation, and human workflow, and makes things actually work together.

My edge isn't being the deepest specialist in one lane. It's understanding enough of every lane to see the whole track.
